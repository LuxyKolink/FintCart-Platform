Softer, shadow-led content card (vs. the bordered ModuleBox). Use for catalog article cards and result panels.

```jsx
<Card interactive onClick={open}>
  <h3>Cómo armar un fondo de emergencia</h3>
  <p>Tres meses de gastos, en pesos…</p>
</Card>
```

`interactive` adds a hover lift + pointer. Use `padded={false}` to bleed an image to the edges.
